Transparency & Mechanics

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use FloatAlpha for free? Yes

Yes. The Free plan provides full access to the Macro Market Terminal, individual Asset Intelligence screens, Price Confidence ratings, core screening filters, and verified 24-hour observation history. It is not a time-limited trial.

What does Pro unlock? Features

Pro unlocks the advanced multi-condition query engine in the Screener, supply contraction indicators, turnover velocity anomaly filters, extended multi-week historical analytics, unlimited custom watchlists, saved screen templates, and full CSV raw data export.

Does FloatAlpha provide investment advice? Strictly No

No. FloatAlpha is an institutional surveillance tool providing descriptive statistical observations over virtual asset inventories. We do not provide buy or sell recommendations, investment advisory services, or speculative financial advice.

Where does FloatAlpha's data come from? Skinport Ingestion Feed

All currently displayed live telemetry is grounded directly in verified snapshot observations from our Skinport API ingestion pipeline across our 100 pilot assets. FloatAlpha transparently indicates when data is accumulating rather than fabricating synthetic order-book depth.

What is Price Confidence? Core Principle

Price Confidence is FloatAlpha's audit of observation quality. It evaluates active listing depth, recent sales activity frequency, and observed price dispersion rather than treating every quoted ticker with equal credibility. It is available in both Free and Pro tiers.

Can I cancel Pro anytime? One Click

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through your account dashboard. You will retain full Pro access until the conclusion of your current billing period, after which your workspace transitions cleanly back to the Free plan.
